## Ranking after round 3

Superseded if the problem statement PDF governs: see [constraints.md](constraints.md) and [round 4](04-vast-data-problems.md).

| Rank | Idea | In one line | Uses MetaMask | First thing to prove |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | OfferSure | Insurance against a revoked job offer, from quote to claim payout | Yes | Crustdata separates a risky employer from a stable one and verifies a signatory |
| 2 | Objection | Turns a lost US government contract into a size protest ready for counsel | No | Company data exposes parents and size for real small-business awardees |
| 3 | Proof of Team | Runs an exchange's reviews of listed tokens | Yes | Team data exists for 6 of 8 test tokens and clears HYPE |
| 4 | Outgrown | Recovers revenue from customers who outgrew a discounted plan | Optional | Dated funding rounds and headcount history exist |
| 5 | Successor | Finds where a vanished debtor's founders and staff regrouped | No | A known successor is found without flagging an innocent one |

The ranking is a judgement against the rubric (execution 25, facts 20, robustness 20, value and idea 20, demo 15), not a measured score.
"No competitor found" always means a few quick searches, not proof that none exists.
