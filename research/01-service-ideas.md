# Round 1: service-replacement ideas

| Idea | Request to finished work | Who pays | Main risk | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outgrown | Customer list and plan terms to dated breach, notice and draft invoice | SaaS finance, revenue-recovery consultants | Profile headcount is not payroll | Shortlisted |
| Successor | Debtor name to successor evidence and a brief for a lawyer | Creditors, collection firms | Accusing an honest new startup | Backup |
| Clawback | Job-linked grant to promised vs. visible jobs and an audit notice | Grant agencies, auditors | Undercounts blue-collar staff | Parked |
| Novation | Contracts to acquired or renamed customers and consent papers | Legal ops, law firms | Contract parsing, quiet demo | Parked |
| True-Up | License terms to companies over the free limit and draft quotes | Software vendors, license auditors | A job post does not prove use | Parked |
| Impostor | Staff roster to false employment claims and takedown drafts | Brand-protection firms | Privacy, stale profiles | Parked |
| Vendor verification desk | Vendor name to verification case file | Procurement | It is handbook sample 5.1 | Rejected: obvious |
| Backdoor-hire recovery | Candidate submissions to fee invoice | Recruiting agencies | Existing products | Rejected: crowded |
| Expert and arbitrator conflicts | Names to conflict report | Law firms | Existing products | Rejected: crowded |

## Outgrown

- Match each customer, find when it crossed a limit, apply the contract, then draft the notice and test-mode invoice.
- Company Enrich documents dated funding rounds and a headcount time series. Whether they are populated is untested.
- Headcount alone never triggers an invoice. Parents, namesakes and near-threshold cases are cleared or held.
