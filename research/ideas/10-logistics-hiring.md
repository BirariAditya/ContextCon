# 10 · Logistics hiring

**Track:** Hiring.

## Variant A: layoff-triggered sourcing
Spot rival logistics companies whose headcount just dropped (Crustdata headcount trends), then shortlist their ops and warehouse managers for the client's open roles.
- **Output:** a ranked shortlist with sourced reasons and a booked first call (sandbox).

## Variant B: logistics CV checker
Check each CV line ("Hub Manager, Rivigo, 2023–26") against person and company records.
- **Output:** **Verified / Contradicted / Cannot verify** per line.

## Dumb-but-useful
"Is this candidate's last company still alive?", a one-question agent that catches fake or shut-down employers.

## Risks
- Heavy personal data, which makes the ethics answer harder.
- The no-contact rule limits the demo.
