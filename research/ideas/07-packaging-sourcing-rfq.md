# 07 · Packaging & pallet sourcing for a new warehouse

**Track:** Supply chain.
**One line:** Find real corrugated-box, pallet or stretch-film makers near a new warehouse, verify they exist, prepare RFQs (requests for quotes) for approval, compare the quotes and recommend an order.

## Niche variant
Fabricators of reefer boxes (refrigerated cargo bodies) for a cold-chain expansion.

## Request → result
**Input:** "We open a Hosur warehouse next month and need 5,000 boxes a month."
**Output:**
- a verified supplier shortlist (company real, active, location, capacity signals);
- RFQ drafts held for approval (sandbox recipients only);
- a quote comparison and recommended order.

## Hard cases
- A listing-only "supplier" that is really a trader → flagged.
- Two listings for the same company under different names → merged.

## Risks
Low novelty; real quotes can't come back on the day, so the demo needs role-played replies.
