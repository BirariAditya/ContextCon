# 11 · Acquisition scout & partner pitch checker

**Track:** Investing.

## Variant A: acquisition scout
Find small regional cold-chain or EV last-mile startups likely to raise money or be acquired, ranked by hiring velocity and funding signals, for the 3PL's corporate development team.

## Variant B: partner pitch checker
A carrier or partner claims "500 trucks, 12 cities". Check each claim against headcount, job posts, office locations and news.
- **Output:** **Verified / Contradicted / Cannot verify** per claim.

## Risks
- A 3PL client is an odd buyer for investing work.
- Crowded at earlier ContextCons (several VC-scout repos at IIT Bombay; a VC scout took 2nd at ContextCon Build).
