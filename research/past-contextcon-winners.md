## Patterns in what wins
- A specific, painful job done end to end, not a dashboard.
- Every claim cites a Crustdata record or a web source.
- A non-placing participant said the winners "were solving something they clearly felt themselves". That favours founder-problem fit (see idea 01).
- Recent editions reward trust and verification: Verified / Contradicted / Cannot verify labels appear in many strong repos.
